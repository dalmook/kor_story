The goblins fled, leaving their treasure. Something had terrified them.
That night, a woodcutter hid inside an empty house.
Goblins tapped a magic club, and gold appeared.
Hungry, he cracked a hazelnut. The sound rang out.
The goblins thought the house was collapsing. They fled.
The abandoned club was now the woodcutter's to keep.
No sword. No charm. Just one tiny hazelnut.
